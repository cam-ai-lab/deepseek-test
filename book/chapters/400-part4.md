# Part 4 — Phases, environments and pipelines

So far we've focused on *what* to test and *how*. Part 4 zooms out to *where* and *when*. Chapter 17
walks the ladder of environments, from the test's own JVM to production, and places ephemeral
environments precisely. Chapter 18 introduces performance testing with a Gatling smoke test. Chapter
19 designs the CI pipeline: which tests block a merge, which run nightly, and what to do about flaky
tests. Chapter 20 pulls everything together into a test strategy — the capstone.
