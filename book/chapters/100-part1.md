# Part 1 — A map before the territory

Before we write a single test, we need a shared language. Testing has more jargon per square inch
than almost any other part of software engineering, and most arguments about testing are really
arguments about words: two people say "integration test" and mean different things.

Chapter 1 answers why we test at all, and gives you a tour of the lab. Chapter 2 introduces the
**four axes of testing**, a framework for placing *any* test precisely — the most important idea in
the book. Chapter 3 dissects what makes a single test good or bad. Chapter 4 untangles test
doubles: stubs, fakes, spies and mocks.
